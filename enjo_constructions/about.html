How We Started

From Social Media to Your Building Site

Enjo Constructions began not in an office, but on social media. We saw Ugandan families struggling with overpriced, impractical home designs that didn't reflect local realities — clay soils, tropical heat, tight budgets, and small urban plots.

We started sharing affordable 3D floor plans and honest construction tips on TikTok and Facebook. The response was overwhelming. Thousands of Ugandans were hungry for practical, local knowledge — not textbook theory from foreign design firms.

Today, Enjo Constructions is a trusted resource for homeowners, builders, and diaspora Ugandans planning to build back home. We believe every Ugandan deserves a well-designed, structurally sound home — regardless of budget.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Affordability First

Every design and tip we share is grounded in what's actually achievable on a Ugandan budget — no unrealistic price tags.

Community First

We grew through social media because we shared knowledge freely. Our community's trust means more than any contract.

Local Expertise

Our Journey

How We Grew

2020

The Beginning

Started sharing affordable home designs on Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities across Kampala. First 500 followers in the first month.

2021

TikTok Viral Growth

A floor plan video for a 3BR bungalow under UGX 35M reached 200,000+ views. TikTok became our biggest platform overnight.

2022

First Consultations

Began offering WhatsApp consultation services. Helped over 200 families choose the right design for their plot and budget.

2023

3D Design Library

Launched our full library of 3D floor plans. Partnered with local structural engineers to validate all designs against Ugandan soil conditions.

2025

10,000+ Community

Over 10,000 Ugandan homebuilders now access our weekly tips. This website launches as a permanent home for all our resources.
